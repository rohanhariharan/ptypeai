Privacy

Typecode runs on your computer. There is no account, no sign in, and no telemetry: the app does not report what you do, what you build or how often you open it.

Four things can leave the machine, and only when you ask for them:

  • Your model provider receives the prompts and context you send it. That is the model you chose, not us.
  • Web search sends the query you typed.
  • Academic search sends its query to OpenAlex or Crossref.
  • A report leaves when you export it to Notion or to a vault on a connected drive.

The engine serves a local API on port 8787 with no authentication, because it is meant to be reachable only from the machine it runs on. Do not forward that port.

This website is static. It sets no cookies for advertising or analytics, and the theme you pick is stored in your own browser.
