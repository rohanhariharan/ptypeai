Privacy Policy

ptype is local-first. The application runs on your machine, does not require an account, and does not send us telemetry.

What stays on your machine. Your projects, requests, files, git history, agent runs and the ledger are stored locally (under your user folder) and are never transmitted to us.

Models. If you use a local model (for example Ollama), nothing leaves your machine. If you configure a cloud model (an OpenAI-compatible endpoint or Anthropic), the prompts and content you choose to send are transmitted directly from your machine to that provider, under that provider’s terms. ptype does not proxy or store them.

Agent connections. Other agents connect to the local engine (by default on localhost) through MCP; nothing leaves your machine unless you configure it to.

Website. We do not run advertising or cross-site trackers on ptype.app.

Your rights. Because local data lives on your machine, deleting the app and its data removes it. For data access, correction or deletion, contact us. We comply with applicable law (for example GDPR and CCPA) and respond to verified requests.

Contact: hello@ptype.app.
