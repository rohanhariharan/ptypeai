Pricing

Everything that runs on your machine is free, and stays free. Paid plans pay for the parts that cost us something to run, and for the licence to use Typecode at work.

  • Free

    $0

    forever

    Available now

    The whole application, on your machine, with your own model. This is the plan you can install today.

  • Spark

    $5

    a month, or $50 a year

    In the build, not on sale

    For one person who uses Typecode every day: priority runs, early builds, hosted models when a local one will not do, and the licence to use it at work.

  • Pro

    $20

    a month, or $200 a year

    In the build, not on sale

    For professional use across several machines: cross-device sync and backup, more hosted capacity for the long jobs, and the licence to use it at work.

Annual plans are two months free: $50 a year for Spark, $200 a year for Pro.

In every plan

These are the parts that run on your computer, so they cost us nothing to serve. They are not a trial, they are not limited, and they are not going behind a paywall later.

Desktop app
Native builds for macOS on Apple silicon and Intel, Windows x64, and Linux on x64 and arm64.
Projects and requests
A sentence becomes a project, a set of steps and a run you can watch.
Verification
Work is not finished until the checks pass. Only a passing step is committed.
Background work
Background jobs, specialist plugins, and one control to stop everything running.
Memory and research
Markdown notes with per-agent access levels, local search with citations, web and academic search, and sourced reports.
Tachyon
The agent mesh, its dependencies and the wire agents use to hand work over.
Connections
MCP in both directions, the local HTTP API, live events and the Table.
Your model
Ollama on your machine, any OpenAI compatible endpoint, or Anthropic. Switch whenever you need.

Nothing to buy yet

Spark and Pro are in the build you can download today, and the engine already knows the plan prices. There is no checkout and nothing in the app is locked, so there is nothing to buy yet. If you already have a licence key, paste it in Settings → Licence key; it is checked on your machine. When paid plans go on sale, this page will say so and the checkout will be here.

Until then, using Typecode at work needs a licence written by hand. Write to hello@ptype.app and describe what you want to do with it.
