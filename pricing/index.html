Plans

Base is free forever and includes the complete local product. Spark and Pro add the parts that cost money to run: sync between machines, hosted models, extra integrations and support. Nothing that runs entirely on your own computer is ever locked.

Pre-launch pricing: for information only. These prices are not final and may change or be withdrawn at any time before launch without notice. They are not an offer, and no purchase exists until checkout is completed. Do not base financial or purchasing decisions on them. Spark and Pro are not yet purchasable; Base is free today.
