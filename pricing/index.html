Pricing

Everything that runs on your machine is free, and stays free. Paid plans pay for the parts that cost us something to run, and for the licence to use Motif at work.

    forever

  • Spark

    a month, or $50 a year

  • Pro

    $20

    a month, or $200 a year

    In the build, not on sale

    For professional use across several machines, with hosted capacity for the long jobs.
