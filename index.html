AI that runs on your machine
Completely on your terms

ptype makes software that keeps your work, your model and your data on hardware you own. Typecode, the first product, files a request, writes real files, verifies them and commits what passes.

Your data remains yours

Typecode does the work on the machine in front of you. The model, the files it touches and the record of what changed all stay on your disk, in folders you can open, back up or hand to someone else. Local is the default. If you point it at a hosted model, that provider sees the prompts you send it — nothing else.

You type and it does

A sentence at the prompt is an instruction to act: read the project, make the change, run the checks. What comes back is work that has already happened, not a plan for it.

How it works

  1. 01

    You describe the work

    One sentence at the prompt, the way you would ask someone sitting next to you.

  2. 02

    It writes the files

    Typecode reads the project, makes the change and runs the checks against what it produced.

  3. 03

    It commits what passes

    Work that holds up goes in as a real commit. Work that does not stops before the branch.

New in Typecode 4

The work is not only faster. It is more verifiable, more reversible and easier to connect to the tools you already use.

Connectors

Finished and failed runs post to Slack, Discord or any webhook. The agent can send a summary when you ask it to.

Skills

Reusable working instructions, built in or written by you. The right one loads for the task, and project rules travel with the code.

Visual checks

Headless Chrome at 390 and 1440. Console errors, overflow, broken images and markup/CSS drift are reported before the work counts as done.

Rewind

Every message has a quiet rewind. Restore the files to that point and keep the conversation as a record.

Project chats

Every project keeps its chats in a folder, one markdown log per conversation, searchable and renameable.

Isolated worktrees

Open a repo and Typecode works on its own branch in a real worktree. Your checkout stays untouched, and only passing work is committed.
