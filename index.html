AI that runs on your machine.
Completely on your terms.

ptype makes software that keeps your work, your model and your data on hardware you own. Motif, the first product, files a request, writes real files, verifies them and commits what passes.

Your data remains yours.

Motif does the work on the machine in front of you. The model, the files it touches and the record of what changed all stay on your disk, in folders you can open, back up or hand to someone else.

You type. It does.

A sentence at the prompt is an instruction to act: read the project, make the change, run the checks. What comes back is work that has already happened, not a plan for it.

How it works.

  1. 01

    You describe the work.

    One sentence at the prompt, the way you would ask someone sitting next to you.

  2. 02

    It writes the files.

    Motif reads the project, makes the change and runs the checks against what it produced.

  3. 03

    It commits what passes.

    Work that holds up goes in as a real commit. Work that does not stops before the branch.
